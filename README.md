### 🔹 Task 4 — Data Storytelling & Statistical Validation

**Repository:** [Task 4 – Data Storytelling & Statistical Validation](https://github.com/bindu-chowdhary/Task-4-Data-Storytelling-Statistical-Validation-Apexplanet/tree/main)

**Objective:**  
Convert analytical findings into a clear business story and validate a business question using statistical testing.

**Business question:**

> Is there a significant difference in average Total Sales between Male and Female customers?

**Hypotheses:**

- **H₀ (Null Hypothesis):** There is no significant difference in average Total Sales between Male and Female customers.
- **H₁ (Alternative Hypothesis):** There is a significant difference in average Total Sales between Male and Female customers.

**Statistical method:**
- Independent Samples T-Test (Welch's t-test)
- Significance level: **α = 0.05**

**Results:**
- T-Statistic: **0.682610**
- P-Value: **0.495011**
- Decision: **Fail to Reject H₀**

Since the p-value is greater than 0.05, the Task 4 analysis concludes that the observed difference in average Total Sales between the two gender groups is **not statistically significant at the 5% significance level**.

**Skills demonstrated:**
- Statistical Analysis
- Hypothesis Testing
- Data Interpretation
- Business Storytelling
- Statistical Validation
- Business Recommendations
